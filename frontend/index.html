About ExoHabitAI

What is ExoHabitAI?

ExoHabitAI is an advanced platform for analyzing the habitability potential of exoplanets. Using sophisticated algorithms trained on astronomical data, we predict which distant worlds might be capable of supporting life.

Our Methodology

Our habitability analysis considers five critical factors:

  • Planetary Size: Optimal habitable planets are typically between 0.8 and 1.2 Earth radii
  • Orbital Distance: The planet must reside in the habitable zone (0.95-1.37 AU for Sun-like stars)
  • Atmosphere: A protective atmosphere is essential for maintaining surface conditions suitable for life
  • Temperature: Surface temperatures between 250-310K are ideal for liquid water
  • Water Presence: Liquid water is a fundamental requirement for life as we understand it

Important Limitations

This analysis is based on our current understanding of habitability. The universe may harbor life in forms and environments we cannot yet imagine. These predictions should be viewed as hypothetical assessments based on Earth-like habitability criteria.
